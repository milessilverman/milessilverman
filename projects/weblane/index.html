01 / The project

An idea with a purpose.

Weblane is my web design business. It builds custom, mobile-ready websites for small businesses that make them easier to understand and contact.

Let people see the work first.

Every plan starts with a free custom demo: a business gets a concrete website to click through before deciding anything. The demos span local business niches, with layouts shaped around each one’s services and customers.

Plans.

Starter$299setup + $25/mo

A clean, fast site with the essentials: services, hours, contact, and a mobile-first layout.

Pro$599setup + $50/mo

More pages, more polish, and ongoing updates as the business changes.

Pro+$999setup + $75/mo

The full build with custom features and priority support.

Work to date.

A four-page rebuild for Contract Decor, a commercial window-covering installer in Los Angeles, and about ten demo sites across local business niches.
