02 / The project

An idea with a purpose.

A home battery can help one household during a blackout. Electric Island asks what happens when those resources can support an entire block. The concept uses automatic islanding switches at homes, transformers, and utility poles to connect local sources of power during an outage.

Resilience at the scale of a block.

Our team developed the concept in 48 hours and pitched it at the Dual-Use Hackathon, taking first place among 60 teams. Afterward, I built and deployed an interactive grid simulator to explore the idea further and reviewed prior-art patents to help define its novelty.

A reason for every home to opt in.

Each island battery holds an emergency reserve of about 10 kWh. The rest of its capacity charges on low-cost power and sells back at peak demand, so homeowners earn money from the same battery that keeps the block running in an outage.
