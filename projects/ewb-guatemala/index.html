01 / The project

Sized for the busiest day of 2048.

Engineers Without Borders USC is working with Vicampanavitz, a community of about 80 households in Nebaj, Guatemala, on a new conduction line from a spring to a primary tank. There is no pump: the line drops 172 m into a river valley and climbs back out to a tank site only 13.1 m below the spring.

Fitting the pipe to the drop.

I modeled the line in EPANET 2.2 on our team’s 95-station total station survey to choose pipe sizes and pressure classes. The survey found 11 m less drop than the satellite estimate, which left an all-2-inch line just short of the 1 m minimum pressure at the tank. The design keeps 2-inch PVC for most of the route and switches to 2½-inch for the last 841 m, which uses about 10% more PVC and leaves 2.3 m of pressure at the inlet.
