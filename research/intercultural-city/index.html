High school senior

Can the design of a place help people connect?

This school research essay examines greenery, seating arrangements, lighting, water features, and other public-space elements as possible supports for intercultural interaction. It brings together published research and discussion of park access in Los Angeles to argue for more intentional and equitable public-space design.

I wrote this literature-based essay as a senior in high school, drawing on published research.

Read the full essay · PDF ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Why this interests me.

I’m interested in the way design creates opportunities for everyday interaction. A bench, a shaded gathering place, or a welcoming public space can change how people use a neighborhood. This essay explores the relationship between those design decisions and the kind of community a city hopes to become.
